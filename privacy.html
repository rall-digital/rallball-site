Privacy Policy

Cooper Rall Football · Last updated 21 November 2026

Cooper Rall Football is made by RALL DIGITAL LLC. This policy explains what the game does with information. The short version: the game does not collect personal information, does not have accounts, and does not track you.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not send gameplay data anywhere. There is no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics SDK and no third-party tracking.

What is stored on your device

The game stores your settings (such as audio volume, the selected side and any control preferences) locally on your device. These stay on the device, are not transmitted, and are removed when you delete the app.

Apple services

If you install the game through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may collect crash reports, diagnostics and usage information under Apple's own privacy policy and your device settings. That data goes to Apple, not to us. If you email us for support, we receive your message and email address and use them only to answer you.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
