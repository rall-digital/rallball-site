Cooper Rall Football

Five-a-side football played by Minis and Fiat 500s against a giant ball.

Support

Questions, bugs or feedback: rall.eric@gmail.com. We read everything.

How to play

Pick a side, choose how many players share the screen, and drive. On iPhone and iPad the on-screen controls appear in your pane; on Mac and Apple TV use a game controller. Up to four players can share one screen, and every match fills the rest of the pitch with bots.

Platforms

iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. Local split-screen only — no account and no online play.
